## 1. Notice of Claim (Sub-Clause 20.2.1)

```
[Contractor's letterhead]
Our ref.: ABC-BOP-NOC-007                                Date: [dd Month yyyy]
To: [the Engineer – name, address per Contract Data]
Copy: [Employer]

Contract: [name / number] – [Project name]

NOTICE OF CLAIM under Sub-Clause 20.2.1 [Claims for Payment and/or EOT]
Event: Unforeseeable Physical Conditions – peat layer at Substation foundation (Sub-Clause 4.12)

Dear Sir / Madam,

1. We hereby give Notice under Sub-Clause 20.2.1 of the Conditions of Contract.

2. On [date], during excavation for the foundations of the 110/30 kV Substation
   (grid ref. …), the Contractor encountered a layer of peat approximately 2.4 m
   thick, which is not indicated in the Geotechnical Report (Site Data, Ref. …)
   provided by the Employer under Sub-Clause 2.5. The Contractor became aware of
   this event on [date].

3. In the Contractor's opinion, these physical conditions were Unforeseeable.
   The Contractor has today also given Notice under Sub-Clause 4.12.1.

4. The Contractor considers itself entitled to an extension of the Time for
   Completion and payment of Cost under Sub-Clauses 4.12.4 and 20.2.

5. The Contractor is keeping contemporary records in accordance with
   Sub-Clause 20.2.3 and invites the Engineer to inspect them.

6. A fully detailed Claim will be submitted within the period stated in
   Sub-Clause 20.2.4.

7. This Notice is given without prejudice to any other rights the Contractor may
   have under the Contract or at law, all of which are expressly reserved.

Yours faithfully,
[Name], Contractor's Representative
```

## 3. Variation Proposal (Sub-Clause 13.3.2) – struktura
1. Reference: Engineer's Request for Proposal No. …, date.
2. Description of the proposed work and programme for its execution.
3. Contractor's proposal for modifications to the Programme and to the Time for Completion (Sub-Clause 8.3 / 8.5).
4. Proposed adjustment to the Contract Price, with supporting build-up (rates from the BoQ / new rates, Sub-Clause 12.3).
5. Impact on performance guarantees, if any (Yellow/Silver).
6. Validity and assumptions; exclusions; reservation regarding disruption to other works.

## 4. Fully Detailed Claim (Sub-Clause 20.2.4) – spis treści
```
1. Executive Summary
2. The Contract (parties, key dates, relevant Sub-Clauses, Contract Data)
3. Compliance with Notice requirements (dates, references)
4. Factual background and chronology (with references to Appendix documents)
5. Contractual / legal basis of the Claim (entitlement)
6. Delay analysis (methodology, programmes used, results, concurrency)
7. Quantum (methodology; time-related costs; direct costs; HO overheads; financing)
8. Summary of relief sought (EoT: xx days; payment: EUR xx)
Appendices: A – Correspondence; B – Records; C – Programmes (native files);
D – Photographs; E – Cost evidence; F – Calculations
```
