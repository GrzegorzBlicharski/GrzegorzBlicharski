# THE FIRM — World, Cities & Travel

**Geography tells the career story:** Gliwice → Poland → Germany → Europe → back to Gliwice at a completely different professional level. Warsaw is deliberately **not** a core location. Germany is the second major world and eventually holds more professional locations than Poland.

> Status: **Gliwice** (morning & evening office, partner floor, exteriors) and **Wrocław** (night establishing shot on the Oder, conference room) are built for Chapter I, plus the InterCity train between them. **Munich** exterior painters (twin domes, TV tower) remain in the codebase for the later Munich chapter. Everything else is designed below.

## Germany
Core: **Berlin** (first German world; where translation ends) · **München** (premium; automotive, executive employment, sports business; trips to Starnberger See, Tegernsee, the Alps) · **Heidelberg** (knowledge, examination, slow intellectual chapters) · **Köln** (media, sport, IP) · **Hamburg** (port, trade, night rain — the Gdańsk corridor) · **Bad Homburg** (quiet wealth, executives, discretion) · **Frankfurt** (the hardest deals) · **Düsseldorf** (corporate Germany) · **Stuttgart** (automotive and engineering — the Gliwice corridor).
Special: Freiburg & the Black Forest · Dresden · Leipzig · Nürnberg (Mittelstand) · Bonn · Potsdam · Lübeck · Baden-Baden · Garmisch-Partenkirchen.

Landscapes are story, not tourism: Bavarian Alps, Black Forest, Rhine valley, the Elbe, North Sea and Baltic, the Taunus, Bavarian lakes.

## Corridors (recurring clients and characters)
Gliwice ↔ Stuttgart (automotive, supply — signature storyline) · Wrocław ↔ Dresden / Leipzig (technology, industry) · Poznań ↔ Berlin (trade, distribution) · Szczecin ↔ Berlin / Hamburg (logistics, employment) · Gdańsk ↔ Hamburg (ports) · Gliwice ↔ Frankfurt / Bad Homburg (corporate clients, executives).
Chapter I already seeds the Stuttgart corridor: the buyer and its in-house counsel, Dr. Ines Kraus, are Stuttgart-based.

## Travel as gameplay
Travel is sometimes pressure, not a loading screen: *ICE Köln → Frankfurt, 70 minutes, agreement v17 arrives, then the client calls with new facts.* Chapter I's InterCity to Wrocław carries the first written advice in German.

## Career geography
Early: Gliwice, Wrocław, Poznań → then Gdańsk, Szczecin → first German expansion: Berlin, Leipzig, Dresden → mid-career: Hamburg, Köln, Düsseldorf, Stuttgart → advanced: Frankfurt, Bad Homburg, München → prestige chapters: Heidelberg, Freiburg, Baden-Baden, Garmisch → elite international: London, Madrid, Zürich, Brussels, Milano. Football clubs and sports bodies are fictional.

## Seasons & city memory
Return to places in different seasons (autumn Gliwice, winter Berlin, spring Heidelberg, rainy Hamburg, snowy München, foggy Gdańsk). Cities remember your status: first visit you follow the partner; later you prepare; then you lead; years on, the client calls you directly.
